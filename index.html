📅 Upcoming Meeting

Friday November 13, 2026 · 10:30–17:00

Fall Meeting 2026

Join us for presentations featuring political psychology work from diverse disciplines by both junior and senior researchers, followed by drinks. This time, we will again feature a poster session, in which junior researchers are invited to present their work related to political psychology. Please feel warmly invited to sign up and spread this call to junior researchers you think may be interested. If you're interested in presenting a poster, please indicate this when filling in the sign-up form (see link below). We are again able to sponsor the printing costs for junior researchers who are in need of this support in order to present their poster. If you're interested in this sponsoring, please reach out by sending us an email.
